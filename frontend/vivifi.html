Where Vivifi fits

It doesn't replace your tools. It replaces the busywork.

Vivifi isn't a Canvas competitor, a scheduling app, or even a replacement for not actually knowing your syllabus. It's a layer on top of the one document that already has everything in it.

vs. Canvas & Blackboard

Those update on your professor's schedule.

Which is often never. Vivifi updates on yours. It's the student's tool, not the instructor's. So when your teacher puts your grade in three different places, or doesn't put tests in your actual gradebook for months, you still have the power to keep track of it.

vs. Google Calendar

A calendar knows dates. Not what they're worth.

It won't tell you that Thursday is worth 25% of your grade, or that your lowest homework already got dropped.

vs. generic grade calculators

No more re-typing your grade everytime it changes.

Vivifi reads the PDF you already have and remembers your grades and weights. You can change info as the course changes, unlike other grade calculators.

vs. your assignment tracker

That's only one part of your course.

It won't compute your weighted grade, or catch the attendance policy, or the textbook you need.

Inside Vivifi

See your semester, live.

Every course, every grade, every next item due, and so much more! Change as the semester changes.

Semester hub

One dashboard, all your courses.

A folder-style switcher moves you between semesters. Each course card shows its live grade the moment you've entered one so you're never going into your next exam blind.

Live gradebook

Weighted math, done for you.

Categories, weights, and entries auto-populate from your syllabus. The query box answers "what do I need on the final to get an A?" with a number and scenarios, and nothing is ever calculated by AI.

Our approach to AI

AI just reads the document.
You keep the decisions.

Vivifi is not a chatbot. There's no thread to scroll through and nothing to prompt. AI shows up in exactly three places, and in each one, it does one narrow job well, and then gets out of the way.

Reading

Review before it's real.

We still believe it's important you read your syllabus. It just needs to keep up with you. Afer upload, nothing is saved and you see every extracted field on one editable screen, fix what's wrong, and confirm it yourself. Every value keeps a record of whether it came from the syllabus or from you.

Grade Prediction

Structured answers, no chance at hallucination.

Ask what you need on the final in plain English. AI's only job is turning that sentence into a formal query. Your grade, the actual arithmetic, is computed by ordinary code, every time, the same way.

Interacting

No chat surface, anywhere.

No message history, no "great question," no back-and-forth to manage. Every AI touchpoint in Vivifi takes a specific input and returns a specific, structured output.

Language models are good at reading a messy PDF and understanding how a person phrases a question. They're not a substitute for verifiable arithmetic, or for a student's own judgment about their own coursework. So Vivifi never asks you to trust a black box for either.

What's actually in it

Everything your syllabus was supposed to do.

Four main systems, all driven by the same document you already have.

Live Syllabus

Upload a PDF once. Vivifi extracts grade weights, assignments, exams, instructors, office hours, schedules, materials, and everything else your prof hides in there. You review and edit before anything is saved.

Live Gradebook

Weighted math across categories, auto-populated from your assignments plus room for attendance and extra credit. Ask what you need. Get a number back.

Assignment & Exam Tracker

See it in a calendar, week, or list; whatever way your mind works. All your assignments are in one place and in each course.

Instructors & Office Hours

Every professor or TA listed with their own hours, email, and location. See it on a calendar view with your own schedule and with all the other office hours your other professors hold.
